How It Works

  • Go to My Pick, choose your name and select 4 golfers. Once submitted, your picks lock.
  • 1 pick from OWGR Top 5, 2 picks from OWGR 6–25, 1 pick from OWGR 25+. Tiers come from the live world ranking.
  • You can only pick golfers who are in this week's field.
  • Your score = the cumulative score-to-par of your 4 golfers. Lowest wins.
  • If a golfer misses the cut, their final score = score-to-par after their two rounds + 3 penalty shots.
  • WD (withdrew) and DQ (disqualified) players treated the same as a missed cut.
  • Scores update automatically every 2 minutes.
