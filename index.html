How It Works

  • 11 entries, each picks 4 golfers.
  • 1 pick from OWGR 1–5, 2 picks from OWGR 6–25, 1 pick from OWGR 26+.
  • Your score = the cumulative score-to-par of your 4 golfers. Lowest wins.
  • If a golfer misses the cut, their final score = R2 score-to-par + 3 penalty shots.
  • WD (withdrew) and DQ (disqualified) players treated the same as a missed cut.
  • Scores update automatically every 2 minutes.
